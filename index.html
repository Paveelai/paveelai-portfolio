The Virtual Pipeline & 24-Hour Cycle

Pan-Regional Art Direction

To serve Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, and regional South American distributors from a single asset suite, location scouting had to be strategic. Workshop spaces, street architecture, and coastal plates were selected to reflect a shared modern South American aesthetic instead of identifiable landmarks tied to one country.

Concept to Storyboard

  • Mapped vehicle engineering highlights to real-world tasks for small business owners.
  • Built lighting and environment moodboards for client alignment.
  • Created shot-by-shot broadcast storyboards detailing camera angles, talent blocking, and payload capacity features.

Realism by Addition

Standard automotive retouching is subtractive: removing rigs, reflections, and road grit. A virtual CGI shoot is the exact opposite. Pure renders look sterile, so realism had to be added back manually:

  • Directed the CGI team to compress the tires against the asphalt so the vehicle sat with genuine road weight.
  • Integrated subtle atmospheric haze, authentic light spill onto the body panels, and motion blur.

The 24-Hour "Follow-the-Sun" Workflow

Production ran continuously across opposite sides of the world. The 3D team in the Americas rendered and shipped files at the end of their day. Our art team reviewed passes, marked up frames, and adjusted color grading during our daytime hours. Notes arrived in the Americas at their morning call, ready to implement with zero lost time.

Know Your Chevrolet: How Things Work

Client: Chevrolet (Global)
Date: Mar 2022 - Jan 2024
Platform: Official Channel: YouTube Playlist (46 Episodes)

Objective

Reclaim owner education from inconsistent third-party dealer clips. Build an official, branded video library explaining EV features, digital cockpit displays, and active safety systems.

Strategy

Replace expensive live shoots and dense manuals with a modular motion system. Instead of featuring a single car model, we designed an archetypal vehicle with Chevrolet DNA. This kept the content evergreen across model years and allowed GM to scale the framework to Buick, Cadillac, and GMC.

Execution

Directed 46 animated guides on Chevrolet's official channel. Recreated touchscreen menus and vehicle tech as clean vector graphics, turning complex engineering into fast, step-by-step visual answers.

A 46-Episode Scalable Motion System

The Problem

Drivers struggled with new EV and driver-assist tech, but their only options were 400-page paper manuals or sales-heavy dealer clips on YouTube. Chevrolet needed fast, official answers that kept customers connected to the brand.

Demystifying the EV Transition

A major focus was helping drivers shift to electric vehicles with confidence:

  • Visualized charging workflows, Regenerative Braking, and One-Pedal Driving in clear steps.
  • Mapped app-based battery management and route planning to ease range anxiety.

Model-Agnostic Design & Longevity

  • Illustrated an archetypal Chevrolet rather than a specific car nameplate.
  • Videos stay fresh and relevant even after individual models receive exterior facelifts.
  • The focus stays on how the technology works, not the sheet metal.

Enterprise Scale across General Motors

  • The system was so cost-effective that GM adopted the template across Buick, Cadillac, and GMC.
  • Cut live-action shoot costs across multiple divisions while maintaining consistent corporate quality.

Directing Through the Pandemic Together

Storyboarding & Hybrid Direction

After personally storyboarding the entire master video, I faced the unique challenge of splitting production under strict safety guidelines. Adapting on Set & COVID Protocols

The Pre-Built Trench & Reverse Schedule

When we arrived on set and found the massive train track trench already constructed, our crew adapted on the fly. We smoothly flipped our shooting schedule to work in reverse, capturing the final celebratory group scene first while the set was pristine, and gradually dismantling the track environment as the day went on.

Navigating COVID Protocols Together

Produced during the height of COVID-19 restrictions, the shoot required rigorous pre-set testing protocols and strict, constant masking for the entire crew. Thanks to everyone's patience and dedication, we kept a complex production running smoothly.

Managing Set Energy

Directing a large cast of children naturally introduces unpredictable energy. With the help of a fantastic crew and careful choreography, we captured genuine, spontaneous moments of teamwork.

Concept Metaphor

Translating cognitive science into a relatable commercial narrative required a strong central visual mechanism. Interlocking train tracks served as a physical proxy for neural connections and social cooperation.

Chevrolet Trax 2016 : Unlock The City

Client: Chevrolet (Indonesia)
Date: 2016
Innovation: World's First Ever Virtual Test Drive

Objective

Position the Trax as the agile compact SUV for discovering hidden Jakarta culture and dining while overcoming the ultimate urban test drive barrier.

Strategy

Combine digital livestreaming, mobile game mechanics, and live broadcast entertainment to turn the city's notorious gridlock into an interactive engagement platform.

Execution

Conceived and directed a live interactive celebrity game show where online viewers voted in real time to steer drivers through Jakarta, unlocking city secrets and proving vehicle handling.

Skills & Capabilities

Art Direction Experiential Campaign Live Broadcast Digital Innovation Social Media Strategy Southeast Asia Campaign

The Virtual Test Drive & Jakarta Gridlock

The Jakarta Challenge

Facing a city of 30 million people with severe traffic jams, leaving drivers stuck in gridlock for hours on an average weekday, traditional test drives were virtually impossible. We had to reinvent how customers experienced the vehicle.

The Virtual Test Drive Mechanism

Introduced an online test drive through live-streamed cameras, allowing customers to take control of two Chevrolet Trax directly from their living rooms. Users tracked the cars via interactive maps, sent live comments and challenges, and watched popular comedians on board commentate as they explored Jakarta.

Competitive Gaming & Real-Time Engagement

Pitted two Trax vehicles against each other in a head-to-head live challenge to fulfill incoming user requests, gamifying the traditional test drive and turning active community participation into a dynamic competition.
