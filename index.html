The Virtual Pipeline & 24-Hour Cycle

Pan-Regional Art Direction

To support teams in Brazil, Mexico, Colombia, and regional distributors from one core asset package, we scouted workshops, streets, and coastal spots that felt authentically South American without pointing to any single country.

Concept to Storyboard

  • Mapped vehicle engineering highlights to real-world tasks for small business owners.
  • Built lighting and environment moodboards for client alignment.
  • Created shot-by-shot broadcast storyboards detailing camera angles, talent blocking, and payload capacity features.

Realism by Addition

Standard retouching removes camera rigs and road grit, but virtual CGI shoots work the opposite way. Clean renders look sterile, so we built believable details back in by hand:

  • Directed the 3D team to compress tires against the road so the vehicle felt weighted and grounded.
  • Integrated subtle atmospheric haze, authentic light spill onto the body panels, and motion blur.

The 24-Hour "Follow-the-Sun" Workflow

Production ran around the clock across time zones. The 3D team in the Americas handed off renders at the end of their workday. Our art team reviewed the frames, marked up feedback, and adjusted color during ours, leaving clear notes ready for their morning call.

Execution

The Problem

Drivers felt overwhelmed by new features and had to choose between thick manuals or sales-heavy dealer videos. Chevrolet needed clear, direct guides that answered questions simply and built trust.

Demystifying the EV Transition

Engineering the Parallel Shoot

Celebrity contracts for Chompoo's twins covered Thai domestic broadcast only.
